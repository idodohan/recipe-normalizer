Classic Vanilla Pound Cake

By Maria Wexler · Published March 18, 2024 · 412 ratings

There are a thousand pound cake recipes on the internet, and I have tried what feels like most of them. This one is the keeper: a tight, buttery crumb, a crackly golden top, and a vanilla flavor that actually tastes like vanilla instead of sugar.

The secret is patience while creaming the butter — give it the full four minutes. If you rush it, the cake bakes up squat and greasy. Scroll on for the printable card.

Classic Vanilla Pound Cake

Prep: 15 min · Cook: 30 min · Total: 45 min · Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup unsalted butter, room temperature
  • 1 1/4 cups granulated sugar
  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup whole milk
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 9x5 inch loaf pan.
  2. Cream the butter and sugar together until pale and fluffy, about 4 minutes.
  3. Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition, then mix in the vanilla.
  4. Whisk the flour, baking powder and salt, then fold into the batter alternating with the milk.
  5. Pour into the pan and bake for 30 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean. Cool before slicing.
