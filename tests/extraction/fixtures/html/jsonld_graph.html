Slow-Braised Short Ribs

Serves 6 · Prep 20 min · Cook 1 hr 20 min

Short ribs are the most forgiving cut in the braising repertoire. Wine, stock, time — that is the whole trick. This version keeps the aromatics minimal so the beef and the reduced braising liquid carry the plate.

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs bone-in beef short ribs
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 large yellow onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, smashed
  • 2 cups dry red wine
  • 2 cups beef stock
  • 2 sprigs fresh thyme
  • 1 bay leaf
  • kosher salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Season the short ribs generously with salt and pepper, then sear in olive oil over high heat until deeply browned on all sides.
  2. Remove the ribs, lower the heat, and cook the onion and garlic until soft, scraping up the browned bits.
  3. Pour in the wine, simmer for 5 minutes, then add the stock, thyme and bay leaf and return the ribs to the pot.
  4. Cover and braise in a 325°F oven for 1 hour 20 minutes, until the meat pulls easily from the bone.
  5. Skim the fat, reduce the braising liquid, and serve the ribs with the sauce spooned over.
