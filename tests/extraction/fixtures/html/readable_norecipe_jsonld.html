Weeknight Garlic Butter Pasta

Posted on 14 January 2026

People keep asking me for this one after I brought it to the office potluck, so here it is, written down properly for once. It is the dish I make when the fridge is nearly empty and I have exactly ten minutes of patience left in the day.

There is no structured recipe card on this blog and there never will be. I write my recipes like letters. Bear with me.

What you need

For two generous bowls you want the following:

  • 250 g dried spaghetti
  • 6 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 6 cloves of garlic, sliced thin as you can manage
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili flakes
  • a big handful of grated parmesan, plus more for the table
  • juice of half a lemon
  • salt for the pasta water
  • flat-leaf parsley, chopped, if you have it

How to make it

Put a large pot of water on to boil and salt it like you mean it. Drop in the spaghetti and set a timer for one minute less than the packet says.

While the pasta cooks, melt the butter in your widest pan over medium-low heat. Add the sliced garlic and the chili flakes and let them go gently — the garlic should turn the color of pale straw, never brown. This takes about three minutes and you should not walk away.

Before you drain the pasta, scoop out a mug of the cooking water. Drag the spaghetti straight into the butter pan, add a splash of the starchy water, and toss hard over the heat until the sauce goes glossy and clings to the noodles.

Off the heat, add the parmesan and the lemon juice and toss again. Taste. It will need nothing, but add salt if your nerve fails you. Shower with parsley and eat immediately, standing at the counter if necessary.

Notes

Anchovies melted into the butter are excellent if your household allows them. Leftovers do not exist for this dish, so I cannot advise on storage.
