- **[P1] Failure states are machine output; Delete has the weakest affordances of any control**: map job failure reasons to human causes + recovery; distinct ErrorState vs EmptyState; real Delete affordance + named, permanent confirm.
- **[P1] Review gate: unaided blob-vs-form diff; ingredient grid clips at 1440px ("optio…"); Accept below full scroll; no dirty guard**. Fix: fit/wrap grid, pin verdict bar, leave guard, A/R shortcuts; (field-level confidence = backlog feature).
